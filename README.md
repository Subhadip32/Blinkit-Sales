## 🔮 The Core Mystery: The Visibility Paradox

Take a close look at the performance matrix above. **Grocery Stores** generate the lowest overall sales volume ($151.9K), yet they boast the **highest average product visibility index (0.10)** across the entire network—nearly double that of high-earning Supermarkets. 

*Why are the least profitable hubs commanding the highest visual exposure? Are high-visibility placements being wasted on low-conversion locations, or are local grocery stores acting as critical discovery nodes for the brand?*
